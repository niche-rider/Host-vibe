Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers regarding lifetime hosting terms, domain renewals, migrations, and service scope.

What does lifetime web hosting mean?

Lifetime hosting means you pay a single upfront price for your base hosting account capacity for the operational lifetime of the HostVibe platform. You will never receive recurring monthly or annual base hosting bills for that included capacity.

Are there any recurring base-hosting fees?

No. Your initial payment covers your included base hosting package. Separate third-party services like domain name registrations or optional managed add-ons are not included in base hosting and renew separately.

How do domain name renewals work?

Domain names are separate annual registrar services. You can connect your existing domain names for free, or register new domain names which renew annually at normal registrar rates.

How does the free website migration work?

After activating your account, submit a migration request ticket with your previous host's cPanel or WordPress details. Our technical team will safely move your files and databases according to your plan migration quota.

How are site backups performed?

HostVibe performs automated weekly system backups for server recovery. Additionally, site owners can generate and download full backups anytime using the cPanel Backup Wizard.

Can I upgrade my account resources in the future?

Yes. If your business grows beyond your initial plan limits, you can easily upgrade to a higher tier plan (e.g., from Launch to Scale or Scale to Prime) by paying the price difference.

Are professional email accounts included?

Yes. All plans include professional email accounts tied to your custom domain: 5 accounts on Launch, 25 accounts on Scale, and 75 accounts on Prime.

What is the refund policy?

HostVibe provides a standard 7-day money-back guarantee for hosting service fees. Optional domain name registrations or custom service fees are non-refundable.

What support options are provided?

We provide 24/7 technical support via our online ticket desk. Our technical staff helps resolve server issues, configuration questions, and account management requests promptly.
