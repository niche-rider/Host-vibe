Privacy Policy

Transparent guidelines on data processing, contact inquiries, and account privacy.

Information We Collect

When you visit the HostVibe website or communicate with us, we collect information necessary to provide web hosting services and technical support. This information may include:

  • Contact details provided voluntarily via our contact form or support tickets (such as name and email address).
  • Basic web server access logs collected standardly by web infrastructure (such as IP address, browser user-agent, and requested page paths).

How We Use Information

We use collected information solely for legitimate service purposes:

  • To respond to user inquiries, technical support tickets, and pre-sales questions.
  • To set up, configure, and maintain customer hosting accounts and site migrations.
  • To ensure network security, prevent fraudulent activity, and enforce system resource limits.

Cookies and Tracking

This static website does not deploy third-party advertising cookies, behavioural profiling trackers, or invasive analytics scripts. Basic session storage or technical cookies may be used strictly for functional features such as maintaining state during support interactions.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

HostVibe does not sell, rent, or lease personal user data to third parties. We share data only with service infrastructure providers strictly necessary to execute domain registrations, payment processing, or order fulfillment when initiated by the user.

Data Protection Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal contact data held by HostVibe. To make a privacy request, please contact our privacy desk at support@hostvibe.com.

Legal Review Notice

Note: This policy provides a plain-English explanation of data flows for HostVibe static web pages. Site owners should review and update this policy to align with specific jurisdictional requirements (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) upon deployment.
